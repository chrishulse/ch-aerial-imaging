About

Professional Aerial Imaging

I’m Chris Hulse, an FAA Part 107 certified drone pilot and cinematographer based in West Texas. C.H. Aerial Imaging creates professional aerial visuals for real estate, commercial projects, landscapes, and cinematic storytelling.

My approach combines technical flight safety with cinematic composition to create visuals that feel intentional, immersive, and visually memorable.

Mission & Visual Style

C.H. Aerial Imaging is built around cinematic aerial storytelling rooted in atmosphere, movement, and strong visual composition. From land and business promotion to documentary-inspired visuals, each project is designed to showcase location, scale, and emotion from above.

Based in West Texas and available for travel.
